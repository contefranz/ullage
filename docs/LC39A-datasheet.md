# LC-39A datasheet (M1.6 LC)

Compiled from 2026-10-21 for M1.6's track LC (docs/m16/plan.md, "LC: the LC-39A set"): the Mobile Launcher (ML), its Launch Umbilical Tower (LUT) with the nine swing arms and the hammerhead crane, the hold-down arms and tail service masts, the pad's hardstand and the flame trench, as Apollo 8 (AS-503) and Apollo 11 (AS-506) stood on them. `Sources/OrbitScene/Site/LC39A.swift` builds the set from these rows.
