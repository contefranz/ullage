## Text values
p. ii (PDF 3) abstract: modified trim-wind-tunnel initial trim lower by ~1.5 deg, resultant L/D higher by ~10 percent; flight L/D and alpha essentially linear with Mach down to 6.0
p. 3 (PDF 11) symbols: d spacecraft reference diameter 12.833 ft; g 32.1740 ft/s^2; C_A = -m A_x/(q S); C_N = -m A_z/(q S); C_Y = m A_y/(q S)
p. 4 (PDF 12) symbols: S reference area 129.35 ft^2; t elapsed time from range zero (first integral second of range time before IU umbilical disconnect)
p. 8 (PDF 16): CM RCS used ~90 lb of propellant to control roll and damp rates above dead-band limits of +-2.0 deg/s in all rate channels (pitch, yaw, roll)
p. 9 (PDF 17): predicted ablator weight loss ~210 lb; Y-axis cg constant; trim about a plane rotated from X-Z by the Y offset; comparison in the resultant alpha_R plane
p. 20 (PDF 28) conclusion 1: initial entry steady-state resultant trim ~154.5 deg, average (L/D)R ~0.368 (tunnel 1.5 deg lower, 10 percent higher); Mach 6.0: ~153 deg and ~0.410 (tunnel 0.5 deg higher, 3 percent lower); good agreement M 4.0 to 1.35 and below 0.9; transonic deviations up to 10 deg and 25 percent
p. 20 conclusion 2: hybrid data agree down to M 1.2; initial entry high Mach (~38), low Re (~1.0e4); tunnel trim poor above M 18 and possibly M 0.9-1.20
