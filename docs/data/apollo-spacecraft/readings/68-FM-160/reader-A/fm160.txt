# Reader A, MSC IN 68-FM-160 (Williamson and Fraley, 3 July 1968), NTRS 200 dpi raster
## p. 1 (PDF 7) summary: jettison of the four SLA panels proposed for missions D (CSM-103/LM-3), E (CSM-104/LM-4), F (CSM-106/LM-5), G (CSM-107/LM-6); jettisoned at CSM/S-IVB separation immediately following panel deployment; 8 fps at 110 +- 20 deg w.r.t. S-IVB +X adequate; orbital abort retrograde SPS 20 min after panel jettison leaves at least 6000 ft clearance
## p. 3 (PDF 9) 3.1 design requirements (ref 2, NR): a. minimum panel rotation rate 33.5 deg/s at deployment; b. hinges release the panels at 45 to 60 deg from S-IVB +X axis to the panel centre line; c. two spring thrusters per panel through its c.g.; d. springs preload ~450 lb, constant 50 lb/in; e. total resultant jettison velocity (rotation + springs) at least 8 fps at an attitude of 110 +- 20 deg w.r.t. S-IVB x-axis; f. minimize recontact
3.2 parametric: jettison attitudes 90 and 130 deg; resultant velocities 5, 8 and 15 fps
## p. 4 (PDF 10): d. average panel weight 593 lb (includes estimated net growth of 20 lb per panel for the spring thrusters, ref 2); e. projected panel reference area 239 sq ft (ref 2), tumbling drag coefficient 1.7 (ref 3); 4.2 panels 1 (+Z, pitched up), 2 (-Z), 3 (+Y, yawed right), 4 (-Y)
## p. 24 (PDF 30): F and G: no recontact with panels jettisoned at 110 +- 20 deg with 5-15 fps; minimum ranges grow to ~40 n mi at pericynthion; bottom panel at 130 deg with 5 fps passes ~1500 ft in front of the CSM/LM; conclusion 1: 5-15 fps at theta = 110 +- 20 deg adequate for D, E, F, G
