## CHECKS AND SUSPECTED INCONSISTENCIES (reader B)
INCONSISTENCY | p-005 says the 8600 lb slosh mass of Figure 4 "is equivalent to the propellant mass for Apollo 8", but Table I gives Apollo 8 propellant as 95 x 100 = 9500 lb.
CONSISTENT | p-003 Apollo 10 "about 60 ft/sec" = Table I derived 60; p-003 "about 345 ft/sec" = Table I Apollo 10 expected 345 (the text calls it a "minimum separation velocity ... for a stable service module"); Figure 3 slosh mass 3300 lb = Apollo 10 propellant 33 x 100 lb.
NOTE | p-003 implies skip-out needs ~90 ft/s (60 + 30). Table I expected value for Apollo 8 is only 78 ft/s, below that, although the text lists Apollo 8 among SMs that "did not skip out as expected".
INCONSISTENCY (minor) | p-003 says C-band tracking on "Apollo 7 and Apollo 10"; Table I also has a C-band-derived value (note c) for Apollo 9 (134 ft/s, above the expected 132).
NOTE | Table I expected dV is not monotonic in propellant (7100 lb -> 275, 9500 -> 78, 2400 -> 132, 3300 -> 345, 4500 -> 360), so it must reflect mission-specific SM mass/sequence, not propellant alone.
CONSISTENT | new sequence roll jets 2 s -> 4 s = 2.0 s on-time (p-005, p-006); -X off at 25 s = 25 s of X thrusting (p-005, p-006); old sequence roll from 2 s for 5 1/2 s (p-003).
NOTE | p-005 "6.0 ft/sec" for a full SM is clearly printed with a decimal point (not 60).
